Technical teams can deploy AI. ROAI rebuilds how a Marketing organization works, what it measures and how it proves its contribution to revenue — human-led, Marketing-focused, built to connect every AI investment to a business outcome.

How we rate a tool

Every rating is produced by the ROAI Governance Framework. We assess each tool from its public vendor documents — privacy policy, terms, data processing agreement, security and trust pages, and public certification registries.

We take no payment from vendors for ratings. Every published rating is produced by AI-assisted research and then verified by a person before it goes live.

What we check

A tier can only be as safe as its weakest factor — the most restrictive one sets the rating.

Training

Does the tool train its AI on your data? Never, only if you opt in, by default, or always.

4 bars never trains · 3 only if you opt in · 2 trains unless you opt out · 1 always trains

Security

Encryption, penetration testing, and independent certifications.

10 bars · more is better

Where the company is based

Some jurisdictions limit how far data can be trusted.

2 bars safe · 1 bar jurisdiction concern

Transparency

How clearly the vendor documents its data practices.

10 bars · more is better

Reaching the top level (“Business Ready”) also requires at least one independent security certification (SOC 2 or ISO 27001). Tools that act as agents on your computer must be fully enterprise-managed or they’re treated as Unsafe.
