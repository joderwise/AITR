    How every rating is made.

    Public sources

    Privacy policy, terms, data processing agreement, security and trust pages, and public certification registries.

    AI-assisted research

    Each pricing tier is graded on its own evidence, so “is this safe?” always has a per-tier answer.

    Human verification

    A person verifies every rating before it goes live. No vendor pays for a rating.

    Re-checked on a schedule

    Sources are re-checked and the last-checked date is shown on every tool.

    The weakest factor sets the rating.

    A tier can only be as safe as its weakest factor — the most restrictive one sets the rating. We check training, security, where the company is based and transparency.
